## Variant: Playful Warm

### Design stance
Warna hangat (krem/emas), rounded besar, blob background, kartu melayang, inviting. Vibe ramah & ceria.

### Key choices
- Layout: center hero + blob, grid 3-col kartu, strip teal
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans 800
- Color: krem (#fdf6ec), emas border, teal aksen
- Interaction: card hover rotate tipis, button lift

### Trade-offs
- Strong: welcoming, cocok untuk anak/ortu, friendly
- Weak: bisa terasa terlalu "cute" buat yang mau serius

### Best for
Audience keluarga / anak, first-time ngaji online.
